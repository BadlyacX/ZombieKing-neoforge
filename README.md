# Zombie King

Adds a rare and dangerous **Zombie King** variant to your world — a crowned zombie that, once it spots a player, calls in reinforcements to overwhelm them.

---

## ⚔️ Features

### 👑 Zombie King
A rare zombie variant that behaves just like a normal zombie **except**:
- Wears a golden crown assembled from floating gold blocks — instantly recognizable at a glance.
- **Immune to sunlight** — it will never burn during the day.
- Has a **13% chance** to replace a normal zombie whenever one would naturally spawn (including from spawn eggs).
- Only **one Zombie King** can exist near a given player at a time.
- The moment it targets a player, it calls forth its guard.

### 🛡️ Armored Zombie Soldiers
When the Zombie King engages a player, it immediately summons **7–10 Armored Zombie Soldiers**:
- Fully equipped in **diamond armor** (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots).
- Wielding a **Sharpness V diamond sword**.
- On death, each has a **5% chance** to drop one piece of its equipment.

### 💣 Crazy Bombers
Once **every** Armored Zombie Soldier has been defeated, the Zombie King unleashes its last line of defense:
- **Crazy Bombers** — baby zombies wearing a block of TNT on their head.
- They charge straight at the player and self-detonate on contact.
- Explosion power matches a real **primed TNT block**.
- Capped at **2 Crazy Bombers** per Zombie King.

---

## 🎯 How an Encounter Plays Out

1. A Zombie King has a small chance to spawn in place of a normal zombie at night (or from a Zombie spawn egg).
2. It behaves like an ordinary zombie until it spots you.
3. On first sighting you, it summons **7–10 Armored Zombie Soldiers** to surround and overwhelm you.
4. Cut down every soldier, and the Zombie King sends in up to **2 Crazy Bombers** to rush you and explode.
5. Survive the assault (and maybe collect some diamond gear along the way) to finish off the King itself.

## 📦 Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| **Minecraft** | 1.21.1 |
| **Mod Loader** | NeoForge |

> ⚠️ **This mod must be installed on both the server and the client.** It registers new entity types, so a vanilla/unmodded client will not be able to join a server running this mod.

---

## 🖼️ Notes

- All three entities reuse the vanilla zombie model/texture — the diamond armor and TNT headgear render automatically through vanilla's equipment layers, so there's no visual inconsistency with the base game's art style.
- The King's crown is a purely cosmetic effect (a ring of floating gold blocks) and does **not** grant any armor value — the Zombie King's combat stats are otherwise identical to a normal zombie.
